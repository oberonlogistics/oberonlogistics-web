About Us

Oberon Logistics, LLC is a proud Landstar Business Capacity Owner (BCO) specializing in Capacity Operations. But what does that mean exactly? It means we've taken everything we learned from years of serving the trucking industry as dispatch professionals and applied it to building something bigger — our own fleet of reliable trucks and professional drivers operating within the trusted Landstar network.

Before becoming a BCO, our team worked directly with owner-operators and carriers, understanding the challenges from every angle. We know what it takes to keep trucks moving, what brokers are looking for, and what Landstar Agents need to succeed. That experience gives us a unique perspective — we don't just provide capacity, we understand the entire operation from the ground up.

When you work with Oberon Logistics, you're partnering with a team that treats every load like it's our own. We manage every aspect of capacity — from equipment readiness and regulatory compliance to real-time communication and load optimization — so Landstar Agents have dependable, high-performing trucks when they need them. Our success is directly tied to yours, which is why we hold ourselves to the highest standards in everything we do.

Headquartered in Sheridan, Wyoming with a base of operations in Jefferson, Georgia, we are committed to the same standards of safety, reliability, and professionalism that define the Landstar network. Whether we're hauling food-grade freight across the country or handling time-sensitive deliveries, our team approaches every load with the same dedication and attention to detail. That's the Oberon difference — we're not just another carrier. We're partners who are invested in your success.
