We're Growing — Check Back Regularly

At Oberon Logistics, we believe that our people are the foundation of everything we do. As a proud Landstar Business Capacity Owner (BCO), we are constantly expanding our operations to meet the growing demand for reliable OTR capacity across the lower 48 states. This means new opportunities are always on the horizon for talented individuals who share our commitment to safety, professionalism, and exceptional service.

Whether you're an experienced professional driver looking for a home where your skills are valued, or an operations specialist who thrives in a fast-paced, growth-oriented environment, we encourage you to check back frequently. We post new positions as our fleet expands and as new talent needs arise within our operations team. Our goal is to build long-term partnerships with team members who are invested in their own growth and in the success of our organization.

Oberon Logistics offers a culture of transparency, respect, and continuous improvement. We understand that the trucking industry is evolving, and we are committed to staying ahead of the curve by investing in our people, our equipment, and our technology. When you join our team, you become part of a company that treats every load like its own — because our success is directly tied to the dedication and expertise of our workforce.

We regularly hire for positions including professional OTR drivers, dispatch coordinators, operations analysts, and customer service representatives. Each role plays a vital part in delivering the consistent, safety-first performance that Landstar Agents depend on. If you're ready to take the next step in your career with a company that values integrity, communication, and results, we invite you to reach out and introduce yourself. Even if you don't see a current opening that matches your skills, sending us your information ensures you'll be considered for future opportunities as we continue to grow.
